# YANAKA RAMEN
## Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0

---

# 1. Brand Identity

## About

Yanaka Ramen is a Japanese Izakaya inspired by Tokyo ramen culture.
The brand combines authenticity, craftsmanship, and modern Japanese simplicity.

## Brand Feeling

Warm • Authentic • Minimal • Premium • Welcoming

## Brand Values

- Authentic Japanese experience
- Quality ingredients
- Craftsmanship
- Hospitality
- Simplicity

---

# 2. Brand Personality

Yanaka should feel:

✓ Calm  
✓ Elegant  
✓ Traditional  
✓ Modern  
✓ Friendly  

Avoid:

✗ Loud designs  
✗ Excessive decoration  
✗ Cheap-looking visuals  

---

# 3. Logo

## Primary Logo

Red logo on white background.

## Alternative Logo

White logo on red background.

## Rules

Do:

✓ Keep proportions unchanged  
✓ Maintain clear space  
✓ Use high-quality files  

Do not:

✗ Stretch  
✗ Rotate  
✗ Add shadows  
✗ Change colours
